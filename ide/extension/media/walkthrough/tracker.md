# Open the Engine Tracker

Click the **Symposium** icon in the Activity Bar (left edge) to open the **Engine Tracker**.

It shows, live, from your training rig:

- the **loss curve** (is the model learning?),
- **VRAM** usage and **GPU temperature**,
- **tokens/sec** throughput,
- the run's status — *running · finished · stopped*.

The tracker auto-reconnects if the rig drops, and replays the whole loss curve when it comes
back, so you never lose the picture.
