# Welcome to Symposium IDE

**A gathering of minds — for building, running, and training AI.**

Symposium IDE is your own editor by [Visionary Sparks](https://visionarysparks.in) — a full
VS Code-class workspace with a built-in bridge to the **Symposium** app: run local LLMs,
share your rig, and watch your own models train, all without leaving the editor.

- 🧠 **Engine Tracker** — live loss curves, VRAM, GPU temperature and tokens/sec from your
  training rig, in the sidebar.
- ⚡ **Start a run** — kick off training on your gaming PC with one command.
- 🧩 **OpenVSX marketplace** — install the extensions you already love.

Use the steps on the left to get set up in under a minute.
